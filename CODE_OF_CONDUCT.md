# Code of Conduct

Home Messages welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities,
experiences and levels of technical knowledge. Treat one another with respect
in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; explain criticism without personal attacks.
- Respect differing experiences and accept reasonable feedback.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances, or publish private
  information without permission.
- Avoid spam, intimidation and repeated disruption of project discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the conduct and its impact,
and the people involved should have an opportunity to explain where practical.

For a concern that can be discussed safely in public, contact the maintainer
through a repository issue. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact
channel without posting identifying details; wait for the maintainer to provide
one. GitHub's own reporting tools remain available for abuse on its platform.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project spaces and to someone explicitly representing
the project elsewhere. It does not require agreement on technical decisions.
